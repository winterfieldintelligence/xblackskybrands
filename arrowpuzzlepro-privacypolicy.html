1 Introduction

Welcome to Arrow Puzzle Pro (“we”, “our”, or “us”), developed and published by Winterfield Intelligence. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through full compliance with this Privacy Policy and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you download, install, and play the mobile game Arrow Puzzle Pro (Package ID: com.wi.arrowpuzzlepro).

2 No User Account & No Login Required

Arrow Puzzle Pro does not require any user registration, login, or account creation.

  • You can open and play the game immediately upon installation without providing any personal information.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or social media profile data.
  • We do not create or maintain online user accounts for this application.

3 Information Collection and Storage

A. Local Device Storage

Arrow Puzzle Pro uses local device storage (such as Android localStorage) to save non-personal game state information directly on your mobile device. This includes:

  • Current puzzle level completed and game progress
  • In-game coins and high scores achieved
  • Audio and game preferences (Music ON/OFF, Sound Effects ON/OFF, Vibration ON/OFF)
Note: This data remains strictly stored on your local device. It is never uploaded to, transmitted to, or stored on external servers by Winterfield Intelligence.

B. Device & Technical Information

The game does not track your IP address, device serial number, contacts, location, or precise geolocation. Standard operating system permissions (such as internet access for basic web view container functionality) are used solely to run the app package smoothly on Android devices.

4 Disclosure Regarding AI-Generated Images

All graphical artwork, tiles, backgrounds, and visual assets used in Arrow Puzzle Pro were generated using Artificial Intelligence tools (including ChatGPT and DALL-E). These images are utilized strictly for game decoration and user interface elements. No user photos, camera media, or personal image files are accessed, requested, or collected by the game.

5 Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Arrow Puzzle Pro is designed for general audiences of all ages and is family-friendly.

  • We comply fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies.
  • We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions).
  • Since the game contains no account creation, login forms, or personal data collection fields, no personal data from children is ever obtained.

6 Data Sharing, Selling & Third-Party Access

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties.

Arrow Puzzle Pro contains no behavioral tracking, advertising identifiers, or analytics scripts that sell or profile user activity across third-party apps or websites.

7 Data Retention & User Control / Deletion

Since all save data is stored locally on your own Android device, you maintain 100% full control over your data at all times:

  • Reset Save Data: You can reset or clear your progress anytime via the settings menu in Arrow Puzzle Pro.
  • Clear App Cache/Data: You can wipe all stored game data by navigating to Android Settings > Apps > Arrow Puzzle Pro > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling Arrow Puzzle Pro completely removes all locally stored game files from your device.

8 Security

We take the security of your device and gameplay experience seriously. By storing game progress locally and eliminating external login servers, we minimize data security risks and ensure a safe gaming environment.

9 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or app features. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Effective Date” at the top of the policy.
